# Voix Horde · Bosphore sur Seine (français)

Ali and Ruşan want our French to sound like **Horde**, the Paris collective behind *Orient Express*,
*Genie* and *Dia de los Muertos* (Cabaret Sauvage, Babour Sauvage). Eliyo has played their nights;
it's the same crowd. Horde writes downtempo / organic house nights as **a journey told like a tale**,
then gives the practical facts in tidy blocks.

This skill describes the style. Never copy Horde's sentences: write our own, about our night.

## 1. The voice in five rules

1. **Open on an image, never on information.** A first sentence that sets a scene or a myth, in the
   present tense: something moves, arrives, crosses. *Ce soir-là, Istanbul remonte la Seine.*
2. **Our myth is the crossing Istanbul → Paris.** The Bosphorus that drifts up the Seine, a vapur
   moored at Pont Marie, the lights of one shore answering the other, a night that leaves from
   Karaköy and docks at the Port des Célestins. Istanbul is the keyword: it appears in every piece.
3. **Speak to the reader as *vous*, with gentle imperatives.** *Montez à bord. Laissez-vous porter.
   Venez comme vous êtes, repartez autrement.* No *tu* in French.
4. **Lyrical, then exact.** Two to four sentences of atmosphere, then the facts, cleanly: date,
   hours, place, programme, tickets. The facts come from the brief only.
5. **Elegance over hype.** Desire is created by images and by scarcity said calmly, never by
   shouting. No exclamation-mark chains, no "incroyable", "à ne pas manquer", "la meilleure soirée".

## 2. Vocabulary

**Use:** Istanbul, la brise d'Istanbul, l'orchestre, le saxophone (confirmed: Michaël Wakko Havard), le son d'Istanbul, le son du Bosphore, le pont, le seuil, la trace, les Balkans, l'Anatolie, les platines, la house, monter, Eliyo, le Bosphore, la Seine, la traversée, l'escale, la rive / l'autre rive, le vapur,
le pont, la péniche, à bord, embarquer, la nuit, l'aube, les lumières, le reflet, le courant, la
dérive, la transe douce, le groove, le set, le live, hybride, les machines, les cordes, la voix,
invité·e surprise.
Genre words stay in English, as on Shotgun: *organic house, downtempo, deep house, live, hybrid set*.

**Never:** saz (and no saz on screen), halay, Turkish rock or any hint at the 2h surprise, folklore, folklorique, soirée turque, ambiance orientale, exotique,
communauté, diaspora, "oriental" as an adjective for people or music, any claim we can't prove.
(Instruments can be *les cordes*, *un instrument d'Anatolie* only if unavoidable; better: say nothing
and let the video show it.)

## 3. Formats

### Instagram caption (feed, Reel)
```
<1–2 lines of image, present tense>
<1 line that turns the image into an invitation, vous>

Vendredi 27 novembre · 20h30 → 4h
Péniche Le Marcounet, Pont Marie
Billets : lien en bio
```
At most one emoji, from 🖤 🌙 🌉. The French block comes first, then `—`, then the Turkish caption.

### Story text (on the visual)
3 to 7 words. A fragment, not a sentence: *Istanbul accoste à 20h30.* · *Dernières traversées.* ·
*Ce soir, la Seine change de rive.*

### Push / newsletter subject
One image + one fact, under 60 characters: *Istanbul accoste vendredi. Il reste des billets.*

## 4. Scarcity and urgency, Horde-style

No numbers we don't have. Ali & Ruşan don't publish tier quantities, so:
- Talk about **the crossing filling up**: *La péniche se remplit. · Les Early Birds ont largué les
  amarres.* (only once Shotgun shows the tier sold out) · *Dernières traversées en ligne.*
- Tickets may remain at the door: say **"S'il reste de la place, au tarif Late, à l'embarquement."**
  Never promise door sales; always push online first: *En ligne, c'est plus sûr.*
- The 2h finale is a surprise: *À 2h, la nuit change de cap. On ne vous dit rien.* Never hint at the genre or a name.

**Last tickets**
> La péniche se remplit, la Seine aussi.
> Dernières traversées en ligne : lien en bio.

## 6. Self-check before handing over copy

- [ ] First line is an image, not an info line.
- [ ] "Istanbul" appears.
- [ ] *vous*, not *tu*.
- [ ] No banned word (saz, halay, folklore, oriental…).
- [ ] Every fact (hours, prices, place, names) matches the brief; anything unsure is `[À CONFIRMER]`.
- [ ] No alcohol, no "boire", no bar imagery (Loi Évin).
- [ ] Read it aloud: would it sit naturally next to a Horde event page?
