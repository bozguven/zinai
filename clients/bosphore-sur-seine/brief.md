## The founders' text (keep its spirit; quote the last line as is)

> Entre les rives, les sons et les cultures. Bosphore sur Seine est un rendez-vous musical et
> culturel imaginé à Paris, à la croisée des influences, des histoires et des scènes contemporaines.
> Un espace de rencontre où les musiques de Paris à Istanbul se croisent, dialoguent et créent une
> harmonie singulière. Des sons d'hier aux grooves d'aujourd'hui, des rives du Bosphore aux nuits
> parisiennes, on se retrouve autour de la musique, du partage et de l'envie de découvrir.
> **Une autre rive, un même mouvement.**

Signature in Turkish: **Başka bir kıyı, aynı akış.** In English: **Another shore, the same movement.**

## The artist

Ruşan Filiztek: saz and oud virtuoso, singer and musicologist. Born in Adana, grew up between
Diyarbakır and Istanbul, in Paris since 2015 (Sorbonne). Prix des Musiques d'ICI 2019. Plays
Kurdish, Turkish, Greek and Armenian repertoire, and has played the Olympia and Les Suds à Arles.
His album *Exils* is built on the Turkish word *sürgün*: both exile and a cutting replanted in new
soil. Birkleyn Sound: his live-session series, filmed with Arpeggio Films.

## Audience (who we write for)

1. **Core:** francophone Turks from Istanbul's French-language lycées (Galatasaray, Notre-Dame de
   Sion, Saint-Benoît, Saint-Joseph, Saint-Michel, Sainte-Pulchérie, Pierre Loti, Tevfik Fikret),
   22–40, studying or working in Paris. Bilingual, stylish, travel in groups, trust their circle.
2. Their French friends, colleagues and partners.
3. Parisians drawn to Istanbul: world-music and Anatolian-rock fans, the barge's regulars.

Past nights by the team drew a halay crowd. This series aims at a curated Istanbul–Paris soirée.
A halay can close the night, but it is never the promise.

## Voice

- **Warm, elegant, a little nostalgic, never folkloric.** Think of a well-dressed friend inviting
  you to a secret spot on the Seine, not a community poster.
- Short sentences. Concrete images: the barge, the lamps, the saz, the Bosphorus ferry, Pont Marie.
- Speak to the reader as *tu* in French and *sen* in Turkish.
- Words we use: rive, traversée, péniche, saz, Istanbul, Paris, la nuit, les lampadaires.
  Turkish: kıyı, vapur, saz, gece, İstanbul, Paris.
- Words we avoid: "soirée turque", "ambiance orientale", "exotique", "folklore", "halay" as a
  selling point, any claim we can't prove ("the best", "unique in Paris").
- Emojis: at most one or two per caption, from 🌉 🎶 🌙 ✨. None in visuals.
